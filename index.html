FAQ

Questions you may have

Do I need a laptop?

No. The programme is mobile-first: every workflow is taught so it runs on a smartphone. In the AI Automation track an Android device can be used directly, and a laptop is optional — never required.

What exactly do I get in the free crash course?

A short, genuinely free practical lesson so you can judge our teaching style before the bootcamp. There is no trial expiry, no card, and no obligation — it does not turn into a paid subscription. The exact lessons are confirmed with the Founder and announced on the page.

How much does the bootcamp cost?

Each track is priced individually, always in XAF (FCFA), and every price includes the 10-Day Bootcamp Completion Certificate. The five tracks are Graphic Designing 6,000 FCFA, Prompt Engineering 5,000 FCFA, Image Generation 5,000 FCFA, AI Automation 7,000 FCFA, and AI Video Generation 5,000 FCFA. Five packages run from 8,100 to 18,850 FCFA. Launch offers are applied only while a confirmed offer window is running, and the active price is always shown on the pricing card.

Are the launch discounts running right now?

Only if a confirmed offer window is live. Launch offers are scheduled in three stages — 20%, then 10%, then 5% off — and each stage only appears between its confirmed start and end dates. When no stage is active, the site shows standard prices and no discount is claimed.

How do I pay?

Every paid enrolment button opens our verified Selar product page, where payment is processed securely. International payments are processed through Selar; the final currency and the exact amount you pay are shown at Selar checkout, where Selar determines any conversion and fees.

Can I pay in another currency?

We list prices in XAF (FCFA) only and do not publish converted amounts. If conversion applies to your payment, Selar handles it and shows you the exact payable amount at checkout before you confirm.

Is the certificate included?

Yes. The 10-Day Bootcamp Completion Certificate is included in every track and package registration price — there is no separate certificate fee. A second, separate 10-Day Challenge Certificate is earned by successfully completing the limited 10-Day Challenge, and is not issued automatically with bootcamp registration.

What is the 10-Day Challenge?

A separate, practical 10-day build where selected participants turn what they have learned into a real, finished piece of work. It has a limited number of places and its own entry requirements, so registering for the bootcamp does not guarantee a place. The exact fee and selection process are announced with the official challenge rules.

Where do I get the lessons and materials?

Registered participants get access to a private Telegram community with dedicated sub-channels for each track, where teaching materials, notes, recordings, announcements and resources are shared. If you cannot use Telegram, the same essential materials are available through a private Google Drive arrangement. Access is restricted to registered participants, and paid materials are not for resale or redistribution.

Can I earn by referring people?

Yes. The ICON Bootcamp Affiliate Program lets you refer qualified paying participants and earn a commission — you do not need to enrol in the bootcamp yourself to take part. The commission rate, payout rules and eligibility are published once the official affiliate terms are confirmed. The affiliate program is separate from normal bootcamp registration.

What are the packages?

Five packages combine tracks into single enrolments: Creative Starter (8,100 FCFA), Automation Starter (9,000 FCFA), Prompt Creator (9,550 FCFA), Creative AI (14,450 FCFA) and Full ICON (18,850 FCFA), which includes all five tracks. Each package price is set by ICON Studios and shown exactly as approved.

How does the website know my payment succeeded?

It does not. Selar confirms your enrolment and sends your access details. This website never claims to verify payments, and we never ask you to send card details to us.

What if I cannot attend live sessions?

Lessons are short and designed for phone viewing, and live sessions are there to unblock you — the practice and projects are what matter. If you miss a session, you can continue at your own pace within the 30 days.

How much time do I need each day?

Roughly one focused hour a day for 30 days. Consistency matters far more than long sessions — the programme is built around short daily practice.

Do I need prior design or AI experience?

No. The tracks start from the fundamentals and assume nothing. If you already create content, you will simply move faster through the opening days.

Can I take just one track?

Yes. Each track is complete on its own and priced individually. The five tracks are designed to build on each other, but you choose what fits your goals and budget.

Who are the facilitators?

Facilitators and mentors are introduced on the page with their real names and credentials as each one is confirmed. Nobody is listed before they are, so you will not see unverified names or invented credentials.

How do I contact you?

Call or WhatsApp +237 675 140 957, or email iconstudiosyde@gmail.com. We answer questions before you pay anything.
